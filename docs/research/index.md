# Research

The evidence record behind the [protocol pages](../protocol/connection.md): how the protocol was studied, which experiments were run, and what is unknown.

## Evidence tags

Protocol claims across the site carry one of three tags and are never upgraded in confidence when moved.

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **verified on hardware** | Observed against a real unit, by capture or by a direct read or write. |
| **from the handbook** | Stated in the manufacturer's Service Handbook or another manual. |
| **unverified** | Not observed on the heater. |
